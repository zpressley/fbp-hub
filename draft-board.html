Changes you make here are saved locally as you edit. When you click Save Board, your list is pushed to the bot repo so Discord /board and this page stay in sync. That sync may take a short delay (Git commit + deploy) before it shows up everywhere.

Discord commands for managing your board:
  • /board – View your current draft board (DM the bot).
  • /add <player> – Add a single prospect to your board (with fuzzy matching).
  • /addmany <players> – Bulk add prospects (comma‑separated list).
  • /remove <player> – Remove a player from your board.
  • /move <player> <position> – Move a player to a specific rank.
  • /clear – Clear your entire board.
