Auction Rules
OB (Originating Bid) — $10 min · 1 per team per week · Mon 3pm – Tue 6:00am ET · OBs win all tiebreaks.

Saturday Match Window — OB manager may match the high challenge bid amount, or forfeit. This is Saturday only.
